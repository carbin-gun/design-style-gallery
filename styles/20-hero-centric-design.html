Core Features

Design Principles

Hero-centric design maximizes impact through strategic visual hierarchy and compelling calls-to-action.

Large Hero Area

Dominant viewport presence with full-screen or near-full-screen hero sections that immediately capture visitor attention.

Compelling Headlines

Bold, benefit-driven headlines that communicate value proposition within seconds of landing on the page.

High-Contrast CTA

Prominent call-to-action buttons with striking color contrast that guide users toward conversion goals.

Dramatic Visuals

Eye-catching imagery, gradients, and animations that create emotional impact and reinforce brand identity.

Instant Impact

Design optimized for the critical first 5 seconds, ensuring visitors understand the core offering immediately.

Mobile Optimized

Responsive hero designs that maintain impact and usability across all device sizes and orientations.

Best Practices

Design Guidelines

Key principles for creating effective hero-centric landing pages.

1

One Clear Message

Focus on a single, powerful value proposition. Avoid cluttering the hero with multiple competing messages.

2

Visual Hierarchy

Guide the eye naturally from headline to subtext to CTA using size, contrast, and spacing.

3

Action-Oriented CTA

Use specific, benefit-driven button text. "Start Free Trial" outperforms generic "Submit" buttons.

4

Supporting Evidence

Include trust signals like customer logos, testimonials, or metrics to reinforce credibility.

5

Performance First

Optimize images and animations. A slow-loading hero defeats its purpose of instant impact.

6

Scroll Affordance

Indicate there's more content below. Use scroll indicators or partial reveals of following sections.
